# Strategic Marketing Plan: CloseClient Elite

## 1. Target Audience (The "Authority" Persona)
- **Primary:** High-ticket coaches (Health, Wealth, Relationships) doing $20k-$100k/mo.
- **Secondary:** B2B Consultants (Fractional C-suite, Revenue Operations).
- **Core Pain:** They have traffic and expertise, but their website is "embarrassing" or doesn't convert.

## 2. Omnichannel Authority Strategy
We will use a "Hub and Spoke" model where all roads lead back to the **Elite Command Center.**

### Channel 1: LinkedIn (The Authority Engine)
- **Goal:** Daily "micro-insights" on positioning and conversion.
- **Action:** Post 3-5 times per week using screenshots of CloseClient Bento layouts as visual proof.
- **Call to Action:** Link to the VSL "Free Training" page.

### Channel 2: The "Big Domino" VSL
- **Goal:** Shift the prospect's belief from "I need a website" to "I need an Authority Engine."
- **Action:** Drive all traffic to a standalone landing page with the VSL script.

### Channel 3: Strategic Partnerships
- **Goal:** Borrow authority from adjacent industries (e.g., High-ticket sales closers, Facebook Ad agencies).
- **Action:** Offer a "white-labeled" Authority Audit for their clients.

## 3. The "Vortex" Funnel (Conversion Path)
1.  **Discovery**: Ad/Social Post/Partnership.
2.  **Reciprocity**: VSL Training ("The Big Domino").
3.  **Micro-Commitment**: The Authority Audit (Modal/Side-over).
4.  **Qualification**: Application form (Custom Form Action).
5.  **Closing**: 15-minute Strategy Call.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Primary:** Total high-value audits requested per month (Target: 20).
- **Secondary:** VSL completion rate (Target: >40%).
- **Tertiary:** SEO rankings for "High Ticket Coach Web Design" and "Authority Architecture."
