# CloseClient Elite Command Center - User Guide

## 🚀 Welcome to Your Digital Headquarters
This theme is engineered to transform you from a "service provider" into an omnipresent authority. Follow these steps to maximize your conversion potential.

---

## 🛠 1. The 5-Minute Initial Setup
1.  **Activate**: Ensure the CloseClient theme and all recommended plugins are active.
2.  **Starter Pages**: Go to **Appearance > Customize > 5. Theme Setup & Tools**.
3.  **Generate**: Click **'Generate Now'** under 'Recreate Starter Pages'. This builds your complete 10-page authority funnel automatically.
4.  **Assign**: Navigate to **Settings > Reading** and set 'Home' as your static front page.

---

## 🏗 2. Customizing Your Authority
### Managing the Homepage
The homepage is modular. You can toggle sections on/off or change their content in **Customize > 3. Homepage Sections**.
*   **Hero**: Use high-fidelity, pain-driven copy.
*   **VSL**: Insert your YouTube/Vimeo training link to pre-sell your prospects.
*   **Bento Grid**: Your Portfolio and Services are rendered as premium "Bento" grids.
*   **ROI Metrics**: Add Key Performance Metrics (e.g., 3.4x ROI) to each Case Study to build immediate trust.
*   **Service Blueprints**: Use the 'Blueprint' meta field on Services to list your core deliverables as a strategic checklist.

### Strategic CRM Integration
You can bypass standard forms and send leads directly to HubSpot or GoHighLevel:
1.  Go to **Customize > 1. Elite Brand Identity > CRM & Form Actions**.
2.  Paste your CRM intake URL into the **'Contact Form Action URL'** or **'Lead Magnet Action URL'** fields.

## 📈 3. The Authority Blueprint Strategy
To land $10k+ clients, your content must follow the **Lead Domino** principle:
1.  **Insights (Blog)**: Share deep-dive systems, not tips.
2.  **Case Studies (Portfolio)**: Show the *mechanism* of how you got the result, not just the result.
3.  **Audit Modal**: Always point traffic to the `#audit` anchor link. This is your terminal conversion point.
