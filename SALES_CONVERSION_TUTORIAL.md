# Tutorial: Engineering Maximum Conversion Velocity

## 1. The Exit-Intent Strategy
CloseClient Elite includes a built-in exit-intent capture.
*   **Logic**: When a user moves their mouse to close the tab, the "Authority Audit" modal appears.
*   **Optimization**: Ensure your Audit Headline (`closeclient_booking_headline`) acknowledges the interruption (e.g., "Wait! Before you leave, check your Authority Score").

## 2. The VSL (Video Sales Letter) Flow
*   **Placement**: Place your VSL section (`section-vsl.php`) above the fold or immediately after the social proof.
*   **Script**: Follow the 4-part script in `VSL_SCRIPT.md`.
*   **Engagement**: Use the "Glass Frame" setting in the Customizer to make the video look like a premium cinematic asset.

## 3. Form Routing (CRM Integration)
*   **For Coaches**: Route all forms to GoHighLevel (GHL) or HubSpot.
*   **How**: Use the `Custom Form Action URL` field in **Customize > Form Integrations**.
*   **Why**: Speed to lead is the #1 variable in high-ticket closing. Automation ensures you never miss a $10k inquiry.
