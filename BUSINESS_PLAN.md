# Business Plan: CloseClient Elite (Authority Architecture Agency)

## 1. Mission & Vision
**Mission:** To rescue world-class experts from being the "best-kept secret" by engineering high-fidelity digital headquarters that command authority and automate revenue.
**Vision:** To become the definitive standard for high-ticket coach and consultant digital infrastructure.

## 2. Value Proposition
Unlike traditional web agencies that sell "aesthetics," CloseClient Elite sells **Positioning & ROI.**
- **The "Bento" Advantage:** High-fidelity visual proof that communicates competence instantly.
- **The "Vortex" System:** Automated lead intake that filters for premium prospects.
- **Strategic Continuity:** All digital assets are aligned with a single "Big Domino" strategy.

## 3. Product & Service Ecosystem
1.  **Revenue Engineering (Tier 1)**: Complete Authority Site build (Home, Services, Case Studies, VSL) - **$15k - $25k.**
2.  **Conversion Optimization (Tier 2)**: Improving existing sites with CloseClient components and Authority Audit - **$5k - $10k.**
3.  **Elite Continuity (Monthly)**: Authority Content maintenance, CRM management, and Performance auditing - **$1,500/mo.**

## 4. Operational Model
- **Platform:** WordPress (CloseClient Elite Proprietary Theme).
- **Tech Stack:** WP Engine (Hosting), HubSpot/ActiveCampaign (CRM), Calendly (Booking).
- **Delivery:** 4-6 week sprints per Revenue Engine build.

## 5. Revenue Projections (Year 1)
- **Year 1 Target:** $250k - $500k.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Target < $2k through strategic outreach and authority content.
- **Lifetime Value (LTV):** Target > $40k (Initial build + 18 months of continuity).

## 6. SWOT Analysis
- **Strengths:** Proprietary theme, deep understanding of coach psychology, hardware-accelerated interactions.
- **Weaknesses:** High-ticket requires manual sales initially.
- **Opportunities:** Massive growth in the "Knowledge Economy."
- **Threats:** AI-generated landing pages (Countered by our "Elite Human Authority" positioning).
