# Premium Offer Structure: CloseClient Elite

To sell $15k - $25k digital headquarters, you must move away from "Deliverables" and towards "Outcomes."

## 1. The "Authority Engine" Package ($15,000+)
*   **The Blueprint**: Complete Authority Audit and Digital Roadmap.
*   **The Command Center**: High-fidelity theme deployment (Home, Services, Case Studies).
*   **The "Big Domino" VSL**: Scripting assistance and high-fidelity video hosting integration.
*   **The Vortex Intake**: Automated lead scoring and qualification system.
*   **The Bento Social Proof**: Strategic transformation of client results into visual authority assets.

## 2. The "Omnipresent Authority" Upsell (+$5,000)
*   **Elite Content System**: 12 high-authority blog articles engineered for SEO and LinkedIn.
*   **Strategic Lead Magnet**: A high-value "Success Blueprint" asset design.
*   **CRM Deep Integration**: Full automation from form submission to calendar booking.

## 3. Pricing Strategy: The "Anchor" Technique
*   **Anchor**: Present the "Signature Ecosystem" first at **$25,000.**
*   **Core Offer**: The "Authority Engine" at **$15,000.**
*   **Comparison**: Show them that a single $15k client closed by this engine pays for the entire investment.

## 4. Guarantee Strategy
Instead of a money-back guarantee (which devalues authority), offer a **"Performance Guarantee"**:
"We will work with you until your digital headquarters is capturing and pre-qualifying leads at your target metric, or we provide 3 months of Elite Continuity for free."
