# High-Ticket Offer Architecture Worksheet

## 1. The Dream Outcome
*   What is the singular, quantifiable result your client wants most?
*   How long does it take to achieve?

## 2. The Unique Mechanism
*   What is your "Golden Roadmap" or "Process"?
*   Give it a proprietary name (e.g., "The Authority Flywheel").

## 3. The Pricing Ladder
*   **Foundation ($3k - $5k)**: The baseline infrastructure.
*   **Ecosystem ($10k - $15k)**: The complete DFY build.
*   **Mastery ($25k+)**: Full omnipresence and recurring advisory.

## 4. Value Stack & Bonuses
*   List every component of your build.
*   Add bonuses that handle the "next logical problem" (e.g., "The Hiring Playbook").

---

## Conversion Checklist:
- [ ] Is your offer positioned as a "One-of-One" solution?
- [ ] Have you removed all "hourly" references?
- [ ] Does your Case Study page prove this mechanism works?
