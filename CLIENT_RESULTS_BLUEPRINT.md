# The Client Results Framework: Engineered for Proof

In the CloseClient ecosystem, the **Portfolio (Case Studies)** is your most valuable asset.

## How to Document a "Mechanism-Based" Case Study
Don't just say "We got results." Show the *leverage point*.

1.  **The Context**: Who was the client? What was their expertise?
2.  **The Bottleneck**: Why couldn't they scale? (e.g., "Trading time for money in the DMs").
3.  **The Intervention**: Which part of your "Unique Mechanism" did you apply?
4.  **The Evidence**: Hard metrics. (e.g., "42% increase in Strategy Call quality").
5.  **The Future**: What is the new baseline for their business?

## Technical Integration
*   Use the **Portfolio CPT** in your WordPress dashboard.
*   Fill in the `Challenge`, `Solution`, and `Outcome` meta-boxes.
*   The theme will automatically render these in a high-authority layout.
