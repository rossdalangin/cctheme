# Elite Sales Page Copy Framework: The "Infinite Ecosystem" Pitch

Use this framework to transform your standard services page into a high-converting sales machine.

## 1. The Call-Out (Top of Page)
*   **Headline**: Direct, result-oriented, and pain-aware.
*   **Example**: "The Exact Mechanism to Scale Your Advisory to $100k/mo Without Adding More To Your Plate."

## 2. The Broken Paradigm (The Problem)
*   Acknowledge why they are stuck.
*   **Key Phrase**: "The reason you aren't scaling isn't because you lack talent; it's because you lack Infrastructure."

## 3. The Big Domino (The VSL)
*   Embed your video training here.
*   Explain the "New Way" of doing things (The Authority Architecture).

## 4. The Solution Stack (Bento Grid)
*   Present your services as "Components of the Machine."
*   Component 1: Strategy/Audit.
*   Component 2: Implementation/Build.
*   Component 3: Optimization/Scale.

## 5. Risk Reversal & Proof
*   Insert your 5-star testimonials.
*   Address common objections via the FAQ accordion.

## 6. The Command Action (Footer)
*   Single CTA: "Apply for Your Authority Audit."
*   Use the Scarcity Label: "Only 3 slots available this month."

---
*Positioning is the multiplier. Execution is the baseline.*
