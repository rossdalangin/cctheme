# Elite Case Study Framework: How to Write 7-Figure Results

To command authority, your case studies must move from "What we did" to "The Transformation Mechanism."

## 1. The Executive Summary
*   **Headline**: [Result] for [Niche] in [Timeframe].
*   **Hook**: One sentence describing the lead domino that was tipped.

## 2. The Bottleneck (The Challenge)
*   What was the "Invisible Ceiling" holding them back?
*   Psychological State: How was the client feeling? (Overwhelmed, invisible, commodity).

## 3. The Authority Architecture (The Solution)
*   Which part of the CloseClient ecosystem was deployed?
*   The "Aha" Moment: What was the primary strategy shift?

## 4. The Scaling Event (The Outcome)
*   **Hard Metrics**: Revenue increase, Lead quality score, Time saved.
*   **Soft Metrics**: Market perception, Ease of sales calls.

## 5. The Terminal CTA
*   Always end with: "Want results like [Client]? Request your Authority Audit here."

---
*Pro Tip: Use the 'Outcome' meta field in the Portfolio CPT to highlight the Hard Metrics.*
