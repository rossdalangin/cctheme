# Lead Magnet Ideas for High-Ticket Coaches

A lead magnet should be the "Missing Piece" of their current strategy.

## 1. The "Revenue Leak" Audit (Diagnostic)
*   **Format:** A 5-minute scorecard or checklist.
*   **Promise:** "Identify the 3 hidden places your coaching business is leaking $10k/mo."

## 2. The "Authority Architecture" Blueprint (Strategic)
*   **Format:** A PDF with a visual bento-grid diagram.
*   **Promise:** "The exact digital infrastructure used by the top 1% of consultants to scale to $100k/mo."

## 3. The "Vortex Intake" Masterclass (Educational)
*   **Format:** A private video training (Integrated into the CloseClient VSL section).
*   **Promise:** "How to automate your lead qualification and stop spending 8 hours a day in the DMs."

## 4. The "Hormozi-Tier" Offer Calculator (Utility)
*   **Format:** A spreadsheet or simple web calculator.
*   **Promise:** "Calculate your client lifetime value and see how much you can afford to pay for an acquisition."
