# CloseClient Elite: Performance & Maintenance Guide

Keep your authority engine running at 60fps with these best practices.

## 1. Image Optimization
*   **Format**: Use `.webp` for all featured images.
*   **Scaling**: Crop images to the recommended aspect ratios (16:9 for heroes, 4:5 for mockups).
*   **Tools**: Use TinyPNG or ShortPixel before uploading.

## 2. Performance Settings
*   **Caching**: We recommend WP Rocket or LiteSpeed Cache.
*   **Animations**: Our `reveal` system is hardware-accelerated. Avoid adding heavy third-party animation plugins (like Elementor motion effects) on top of theme reveals.

## 3. Content Freshness
*   **Monthly Portfolio Update**: Add at least one new Case Study per month to keep social proof current.
*   **Weekly Insights**: Post one high-authority "System" article to your blog to maintain SEO momentum.

## 4. Customizer Hygiene
*   Before making major changes, use the **'Reset to Defaults'** tool in Customize > 5. Theme Setup to return to the "Elite" baseline if you ever get lost.
