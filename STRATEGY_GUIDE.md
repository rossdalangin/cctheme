# CloseClient Authority Theme: Strategy & Documentation

This guide outlines the architecture of the CloseClient theme, the purpose of each page, and how to configure your navigation for maximum conversion.

## 1. Core Page Architecture

| Page | Purpose | Psychological Trigger | Recommended Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Home** | Global Authority | The Big Domino | Hero, Social Proof, Core Services, VSL, Process. |
| **Services** | Solution Mapping | Clarity & Capability | Detailed breakdowns of your frameworks and pricing tiers. |
| **Sales Page** | Direct Conversion | Urgency & Desire | Focused offer, deep VSL, value stacking, and massive social proof. |
| **About** | Origin Story | Trust & Likability | Your "why," your team, and the mission behind the expertise. |
| **Free Training** | Lead Generation | Reciprocity | A standalone VSL page with a single "Apply" or "Book" CTA. |
| **Success Blueprint** | List Building | Micro-Commitment | A lead magnet opt-in page to capture emails in exchange for value. |
| **Case Studies** | Proof of Concept | Social Proof | Deep dives into client results and transformations. |

## 2. Navigation Blueprint

### Header Navigation (Primary)
Focus on the "Big Three" + Your CTA:
1. **Services** (The "What")
2. **Case Studies** (The "Proof")
3. **About** (The "Who")
4. **[Book a Call]** (The "Next Step")

### Footer Navigation

#### Column 1: Branding
- Logo
- One-sentence authority statement.
- Social links (LinkedIn is priority for coaches).

#### Column 2: Solutions (Footer Solutions Menu)
- Authority Infrastructure
- Revenue Engineering
- Vortex Funnels
- Elite Positioning

#### Column 3: Resources (Footer Resources Menu)
- Case Studies
- Free Training
- Blog/Insights
- Success Blueprint

#### Column 4: Support & Compliance
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Contact

## 3. Customizer Management

Every section and page template is 100% manageable via **Appearance > Customize**:

- **Homepage Sections:** Toggle visibility and edit text for all 15 modular blocks.
- **Page Templates:** Edit headlines and content for specialized layouts (Sales, Lead Magnet, etc.).
- **Global Settings:** Control your "Signature Agency" colors and typography globally.

## 4. Recreating Starter Pages

If you need to reset your site structure, go to **Customize > 5. Theme Setup & Tools** and click **Generate Now**. This will programmatically create all the pages listed above with the correct shortcodes and templates already applied.
