# Elite Offer Architecture: The $100k+ Strategy

To sell high-ticket services ($10k - $50k+), your offer must be perceived as a "Category of One."

## 1. The Transformation vs. The Work
*   **The Trap**: Selling "10 hours of coaching" or "a 5-page website."
*   **The Truth**: Selling "The 90-Day Authority Ecosystem."
*   **Action**: Rename your services from nouns (Web Design) to verbs/outcomes (Authority Engineering).

## 2. The Golden Ratio of Pricing
*   **Tier 1: Foundation ($3k-$7k)**: Infrastructure only. You build the engine.
*   **Tier 2: Ecosystem ($10k-$20k)**: Engine + Content + Strategy.
*   **Tier 3: Mastery ($30k+)**: Full Concierge + Perpetual Advisory.

## 3. The Psychology of Scarcity
*   Never show "Unlimited Slots."
*   Use the `closeclient_booking_scarcity` setting to display real-time availability (e.g., "Only 3 spots remaining for July").
