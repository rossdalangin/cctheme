# Omnichannel Content Strategy: CloseClient Elite

To build authority, you must stop posting "tips" and start sharing "systems."

## 1. LinkedIn (Primary Authority Hub)
**Frequency:** 4x per week.
- **Monday: The Case Study Breakdown.** Screenshot a CloseClient Bento layout. Explain the "Challenge -> Solution -> Outcome" transition.
- **Wednesday: The "Big Domino" Refutation.** Tackle a common belief (e.g., "I just need more traffic") and show why Digital Architecture is the real bottleneck.
- **Friday: Infrastructure Deep Dive.** Video walkthrough of a "Vortex" intake form and why scoring leads is better than chasing them.
- **Sunday: Vision & Values.** Share the "CloseClient Elite" philosophy on the future of the knowledge economy.

## 2. YouTube / Loom (Proof & Training)
**Frequency:** 1x per week.
- **Topic:** "How I engineered a $50k/mo intake system for a [Niche] Coach."
- **Format:** Walk through the theme's strategic pages. Show the mobile glassmorphism menu and the side-over audit panel.
- **CTA:** "Book Your Authority Audit at [Site Link]."

## 3. The "Elite Insights" Blog (SEO & Retargeting)
**Frequency:** 2x per month.
- **Keyword Targets:** "High-ticket coaching web design," "Authority Architecture for Consultants," "Revenue Engineering systems."
- **Strategy:** Long-form pillar content that links back to the VSL landing page.

## 4. Newsletter (The Circle)
**Frequency:** Weekly (Saturday morning).
- **Format:** One strategic insight + One success story + One clear CTA to the Audit.
- **Goal:** Nurture leads who watched the VSL but didn't book the audit yet.
