# Client Results Framework: Engineering The Bento Case Study

To command authority, your success stories must be structured as strategic transformations, not just testimonials. Use this framework for the CloseClient Portfolio CPT.

## 1. The "Pre-State" (The Challenge)
*   **Target Pain**: Identify the specific "Complexity Ceiling" or revenue stall the client was facing.
*   **Psychological Hook**: Describe the emotional frustration (e.g., "Burnout from manual DM outreach," "Invisible expert syndrome").
*   **Quantifiable Gap**: The measurable loss (e.g., "Leaking $20k/mo in un-followed leads").

## 2. The "Internal Shift" (The Authority Architecture)
*   **The Big Domino**: What belief did we change? (e.g., Shifted from "more traffic" to "better qualifying").
*   **The CloseClient Solution**: Which components were deployed? (e.g., "VSL Vortex," "Bento Social Proof Grid").
*   **The Unique Mechanism**: How did we solve it differently?

## 3. The "Post-State" (The Outcome)
*   **The Headline Metric**: Lead with the big number (e.g., "$250k in 90 days," "42% increase in call quality").
*   **The Freedom Metric**: The personal win for the coach (e.g., "Removed from daily operations," "Zero manual prospecting").
*   **The Authority Lock**: How this positioned them as the #1 choice in their niche.

## 4. Visual Assets (Bento Engineering)
*   **The Hero Image**: High-fidelity screenshot of their new Command Center.
*   **The Proof Point**: A radial-glow card with the primary growth percentage.
*   **The Narrative**: A short, punchy excerpt that sells the transformation in < 25 words.
