# Strategic Lead Magnet Framework: Pre-Selling the Machine

A lead magnet should not just provide "value"; it should prove that your *specific* system is the only way to solve the client's problem.

## Type 1: The Calculator (ROI Proof)
*   **Concept**: A spreadsheet that calculates the revenue lost by having a low-converting site.
*   **The Bridge**: "If your site converts at 0.5% instead of 3%, you are losing $400k/year."

## Type 2: The Audit Checklist (The Gap)
*   **Concept**: A 20-point checklist of "Authority Components."
*   **The Bridge**: They realize they are missing 18 out of 20 items. You are the only person who builds all 20.

## Type 3: The Case Study Breakdown (The Roadmap)
*   **Concept**: A PDF showing exactly how you took a client from $10k to $100k.
*   **The Bridge**: "I can do the same for you. Apply for an audit."

## How to Integrate in CloseClient
1.  Upload your Lead Magnet PDF to the Media Library.
2.  Go to **Customize > 3. Homepage Sections > Lead Magnet Section**.
3.  Set the Headline and Subheadline.
4.  Optionally, use the **'Lead Magnet Action URL'** to send them to a CRM landing page after they click.
