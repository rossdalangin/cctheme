# Client Onboarding Checklist: The Authority Sprint

A 4-6 week process to take a coach from "Fragmented" to "Elite."

## Phase 1: Strategy & Intelligence (Week 1)
- [ ] **Authority Audit Execution**: Map the client's current gaps.
- [ ] **Core Messaging Pillar Definition**: Define their "Big Domino" belief shift.
- [ ] **VSL Scripting**: Assist the client in recording their training using the `VSL_SCRIPT.md` framework.
- [ ] **Visual Identity Alignment**: Select a Color Scheme Preset (Deep Onyx, Midnight Gold, etc.).

## Phase 2: Architecture & Content (Weeks 2-3)
- [ ] **Starter Page Generation**: Use the 'Generate Now' utility.
- [ ] **Strategic Copywriting**: Replace all placeholder text with Hormozi-tier, outcome-focused copy.
- [ ] **Bento Proof Engineering**: Transform their raw results into high-fidelity portfolio items.
- [ ] **Service Solution Mapping**: Break down their methodology into 3-4 distinct "Capabilities."

## Phase 3: Systems & Vortex (Week 4)
- [ ] **Lead Intake Integration**: Map the side-over panel to their CRM (HubSpot/GHL).
- [ ] **Automation Testing**: Ensure the "Authority Audit" triggers the correct email sequence.
- [ ] **Exit Intent Configuration**: Calibrate the FAB threshold and exit-intent sensitivity.

## Phase 4: Launch & Activation (Week 5)
- [ ] **Go-Live Audit**: 100% check of links, mobile responsiveness, and JSON-LD schema.
- [ ] **Authority Announcement**: Assist client with a LinkedIn post "Launching my new Digital Command Center."
- [ ] **The "Vortex" Handover**: Train the client's team on how to manage the new intake flow.
