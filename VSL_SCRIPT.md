# Video Sales Letter (VSL) Script: The "Big Domino" Strategy

**Target Audience:** High-ticket coaches and consultants making $10k-$30k/mo but stuck in the "hustle trap."
**Goal:** Book an Authority Audit.

---

## 1. The Hook (0:00 - 0:45)
*(Visual: High-energy B-roll of luxury offices, clean typography)*

"Most coaches are one referral drought away from bankruptcy. You’ve built a great business, you have world-class results, but you’re still spending 8 hours a day in the DMs or relying on the 'hope and pray' method for your next client."

"The truth? You don't have a traffic problem. You have an **Authority Infrastructure** problem."

## 2. The Problem & Agitation (0:45 - 2:00)
"You’re being perceived as a 'service provider' instead of an 'executive advisor.' When you look like everyone else, you have to compete on price. When you command authority, you dictate the terms."

"If your website looks like a passive brochure, it’s costing you millions in lost opportunity. It should be your top-performing associate: pre-qualifying, positioning, and closing premium clients before you even get on the call."

## 3. The Solution: The Authority Engine (2:00 - 4:00)
"We build what we call the 'Elite Authority Engine.' A three-pillar system designed for the 1% of experts."

*   **Pillar 1: Vortex Intake.** No more tire-kickers. We build the filtering systems that ensure only pre-sold, high-value leads reach your calendar.
*   **Pillar 2: Bento Architecture.** A high-fidelity visual system that proves your dominance instantly.
*   **Pillar 3: Conversion Engineering.** Psychological triggers that turn cold visitors into 'ready-to-pay' partners.

## 4. Social Proof & Case Studies (4:00 - 5:30)
"This is the exact same system we used to help Sarah Chen scale from $20k to $100k months in under 90 days. It’s the same framework Julian Close used to automate his agency's lead flow entirely."

## 5. The Call to Action (5:30 - End)
"I’m looking for 3 established experts this month who are ready to graduate from 'hustling' to 'owning a machine.'"

"Click the button below to apply for your **Authority Audit**. We’ll deep-dive into your current funnel, find the leaks, and hand you the roadmap to $100k months."

"Stop begging for leads. Start commanding authority."
