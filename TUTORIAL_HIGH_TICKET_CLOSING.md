# High-Ticket Closing Framework: The "Authority Audit" Call

This guide helps you transform standard "sales calls" into "Strategy Audits" that justify $10k - $50k investments.

## Phase 1: The Pre-Audit (Positioning)
*   Ensure the prospect has watched your VSL.
*   The goal isn't to "sell," but to "diagnose."
*   **The Mindset**: You are the Architect. They are the Client seeking a Blueprint.

## Phase 2: The Diagnostic (Discovery)
1.  **The Goal**: "What is the absolute revenue target for the next 12 months?"
2.  **The Gap**: "What is specifically stopping you from hitting that now?"
3.  **The Cost of Inaction**: "If nothing changes, where will the business be in 2 years?"

## Phase 3: The Gap Analysis (Prescription)
*   Relate their problems back to the "Authority Architecture" components (from your Bento Grid).
*   "Your current site is a 'Generalist' site. You need a 'Specialist' Infrastructure."

## Phase 4: The Investment (Closing)
*   Don't pitch "Web Design." Pitch "The Scalable Engine."
*   **Price Presentation**: "The investment to install this engine is $15,000. We typically see ROI within the first 2 clients landed via the system."

## Phase 5: The Commitment
*   Ask: "Is this the system you want to use to hit your $100k/mo goal?"

---
*Close the gap, not the lead.*
